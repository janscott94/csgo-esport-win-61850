## Overview

Counter-Manager is an HTML5 esports management simulation designed around the competitive world of CS:GO and CS2. Players can create a custom organization, assemble a roster, assign five-role positions, adjust player attributes, and guide their club through matches and major tournament stages.

The simulation combines team operations with match and business systems. A round-based economy, live kill feed, transfer market, club finances, and HLTV-inspired world rankings give each campaign multiple areas to manage. Progress is stored in the browser through LocalStorage, allowing a saved management career to continue between sessions.
